EchoYeast · SZPU-CHINA

SZPU-China is a student team from Shenzhen Polytechnic University competing in iGEM 2026. We build a yeast-cell POCT biosensor for rapid on-site detection of influenza A virus, based on the programmable antigen-gated receptor (PAGER) system.
