 Cross-Team Collaboration

 Our team operates through integrated cross-functional collaboration. Wet lab members provide experimental
 data that informs dry lab modeling, while human practices activities are integrated into the project's
 design cycle. This interdisciplinary approach ensures that our engineering solutions are grounded in both
 scientific rigor and real-world applicability.

 Modeling software and services

 Redistributed here: 
 No (external tools) 

 Used for 
 The docking, refinement and simulation results reported on the Model page were produced with the following programs and web services, each under its own license and terms:

 ClusPro 2.0 &ndash; rigid docking web server (Boston University): cluspro.bu.edu 

 HADDOCK 2.5 &ndash; semi-flexible refinement server (Bonvin Lab, Utrecht University): wenmr.science.uu.nl/haddock2.4 

 GROMACS &ndash; molecular dynamics engine: gromacs.org

 Structure prediction &ndash; the predicted nanobody models; the exact tool and version are recorded in the modeling document and are still being verified against the laboratory record.
