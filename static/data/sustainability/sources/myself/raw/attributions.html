Cross-Team Collaboration

Our team operates through integrated cross-functional collaboration. Wet lab members provide experimental data that informs dry lab modeling, while human practices activities are integrated into the project's design cycle. This interdisciplinary approach ensures that our engineering solutions are grounded in both scientific rigor and real-world applicability.

Modeling software and services

Redistributed here: No (external tools)
Used for

The docking, refinement and simulation results reported on the Model page were produced with the following programs and web services, each under its own license and terms:

  • ClusPro 2.0 – rigid docking web server (Boston University): cluspro.bu.edu
  • HADDOCK 2.5 – semi-flexible refinement server (Bonvin Lab, Utrecht University): wenmr.science.uu.nl/haddock2.4
  • GROMACS – molecular dynamics engine: gromacs.org
  • gmx_MMPBSA – binding free-energy estimation: gmx_MMPBSA
  • PyMOL and SPICE – interface and interaction analysis (pymol.org)
  • Structure prediction – the predicted nanobody models; the exact tool and version are recorded in the modeling document and are still being verified against the laboratory record.

The viewer shows trimmed copies of the public RCSB PDB entries 9VH4, 9VH5 and 9MQ2; the applicable data-usage terms are those of the PDB archive.
