Notebook

These notebooks serve as the main entry for our wet-lab documentation of the yeast-based biosensor targeting airborne influenza A virus (IAV). Our PAGER (Protein-fragment Assisted GPCR Expression Reporter) antigen-gated receptor system is built in four independent functional modules. Below you can access experimental records for chassis engineering, humanized G-protein chimera construction, PAGER fusion-receptor assembly, and reporter-module validation respectively. Under each record, the protocols cited in that document are listed by number as they appear in the Protocol page, and each number opens that protocol directly.

Chassis Engineering

This notebook records wet-lab procedures for chassis optimization of our yeast biosensor. We performed CRISPR-Cas9-mediated knockout of endogenous FAR1 and SST2 in Saccharomyces cerevisiae BY4741, aiming to eliminate cell-cycle arrest and G-protein-signaling negative feedback, so as to improve signal sensitivity for our downstream PAGER antigen-gated receptor system.

STE2 Gating Engineering

This notebook records the STE2 gating engineering track, in which the native α-factor receptor was redesigned into an antigen-gated surface receptor: the gating construct (signal peptide–antagonist–anti-H1N1_HA nanobody–ALFA tag–TEVcs) and its sequence synthesis, amplification of the STE2 upstream and downstream homology arms, sgRNA and repair-template assembly, isolation of the gating-engineered strains, the STE2–yEGFP fusion used for membrane-localisation checks, protein-expression detection, and the functional validation runs.
