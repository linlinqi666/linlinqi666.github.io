Parts

Overview

This collection gathers the biological parts PAGER-Yeast contributed to the iGEM Registry. They cover the re-engineered Saccharomyces cerevisiae chassis, the humanised G-protein interface, the reporter that reads the pathway out, and the antigen-recognition module that gates the receptor; below they are listed the way the Registry itself classifies them, as basic parts and composite parts.

23 parts — 17 basic, 6 composite.

Every part number links to its Registry page, where the full sequence, annotations and any future characterisation data are kept.

How this collection is organised

Every entry below is one Registry part, and the two sections follow the Registry classification rather than the order of the experiments. Basic parts are single functional units — a guide sequence, a homology arm, the EYNLV swap, a promoter — that can be used on their own or recombined freely. Composite parts are assemblies of two or more basic parts, submitted so that a combination which is known to work can be taken as a single piece. Each composite entry lists what it is built from.

All four modules of the project are represented here: chassis engineering, GPA1 humanisation, the reporter system, and the antigen-recognition module that gates the receptor.

Basic Parts

The single units this project is built from — 17 parts, each usable on its own or as the building block of one of the composites below.

Part ID Name Type Length Function
  • BBa_267ZCE19 Basic part

    SpCas9 sgRNA scaffold (pML104)

    Non-coding RNA
    79 bp

    The constant 79 bp scaffold of the SpCas9-compatible sgRNA used in the pML104 CRISPR-Cas9 system. It supplies the structured RNA region that lets the sgRNA fold and bind SpCas9; it does not decide which locus gets cut.

    Source and composition

    Chimeric sgRNA scaffold taken from the pML104 CRISPR-Cas9 system.

  • BBa_262TZXV3 Basic part

    FAR1-targeting guide sequence (crRNA_FAR1_KO_up)

    Guide RNA
    20 bp

    A 20 bp guide sequence aimed at the FAR1/YJL157C locus of Saccharomyces cerevisiae BY4741. It carries the targeting information for a pML104-based SpCas9 sgRNA cassette, so a FAR1 knockout needs nothing beyond this spacer plus the shared scaffold; the adjacent TGG PAM sits outside the part and is not included here. FAR1 encodes the cell-cycle inhibitor that arrests G1 after pheromone stimulation, which is why removing it keeps the strain dividing through a long fluorescence read-out.

    Source and composition

    Designed in CRISPOR and picked on predicted on-target scores together with off-target analysis.

  • BBa_26HN2W3G Basic part

    FAR1 homology repair template (4 bp deletion, PAM mutated)

    Engineered region
    1142 bp

    The donor that repairs FAR1 once Cas9 has cut it. It carries 4 bp missing (CACT) inside the guide site GCGTCACGATCTCCACTTGG and a PAM changed from TGG to TAG, so the repaired chromosome is no longer recognised by the same guide. The short deletion shifts the reading frame and knocks the gene out; the PAM edit is the safeguard against a second cut. No foreign sequence and no selection marker are introduced, so the chassis stays marker-free apart from later integration steps.

    Source and composition

    S. cerevisiae FAR1 locus, modified with a 4 bp deletion and a PAM mutation on top of the CRISPR target GCGTCACGATCTCCACTTGG.

  • BBa_2658B1ZA Basic part

    SST2-targeting guide sequence (crRNA_SST2_KO)

    Guide RNA
    20 bp

    The SST2 counterpart of the FAR1 guide: 20 bp aimed at the SST2 locus of Saccharomyces cerevisiae BY4741 and used the same way in a pML104 sgRNA cassette. SST2 is the principal negative regulator of mating-pheromone signalling, so taking it out is what lifts the ceiling on how strongly the pathway can respond. The CGG PAM next to this target is not part of the part.

    Source and composition

    Designed computationally with a CRISPR design tool and selected for SpCas9 knockout in the pML104 system.

  • BBa_262SR0ED Basic part

    SST2 homology repair template (4 bp deletion, PAM mutated)

    Engineered region
    989 bp

    The donor that repairs SST2 once Cas9 has cut it, mirroring the FAR1 template: 4 bp missing (TCTG) inside the target site TCGCCACGAGTTCTGTCCTG, and the adjacent PAM changed from CGG to CAG so the repaired locus is no longer a valid target. The deletion shifts the reading frame and knocks the gene out; the PAM edit makes a second cut unlikely. Nothing foreign and no selection marker are introduced.

    Source and composition

    S. cerevisiae SST2 locus, modified with a 4 bp deletion and a PAM mutation on top of the CRISPR target TCGCCACGAGTTCTGTCCTG.

  • BBa_26HUBYAY Basic part

    GPA1 upstream homology arm

    Homologous region
    500 bp

    500 bp taken from directly upstream of GPA1/YHR005C. Its only job is to be recognised: Saccharomyces cerevisiae recombines where the sequence matches, so this arm is what delivers the replacement to the right place on chromosome VIII. It pairs with the downstream arm to bracket the region being rewritten.

    Source and composition

    The GPA1/YHR005C locus on chromosome VIII of Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C.

  • BBa_26EG2LOR Basic part

    GPA1 C-terminal Gαq-derived EYNLV sequence with stop codon

    Engineered region
    18 bp

    The 18 bp at the centre of the swap: five Gαq-derived residues, EYNLV, followed by a stop codon. The C-terminus of a Gα protein is what decides which receptor it can work with, so replacing the yeast KIGII with this sequence is the whole point of the humanisation step. The design follows the Gαq chimera reported by Komatsuzaki and colleagues (1997).

    Source and composition

    Designed from the C-terminal five-residue Gαq chimera reported by Komatsuzaki et al. (1997); C-terminal sequence of Homo sapiens GNAQ (Gαq).

  • BBa_26YEUKXX Basic part

    GPA1 downstream homology arm

    Homologous region
    500 bp

    500 bp from the region downstream of GPA1/YHR005C, closing the other side of the target. Together with the upstream arm it defines exactly which stretch of the locus is replaced, so the edited allele carries the humanised C-terminus and nothing else from the original sequence.

    Source and composition

    The genomic region downstream of the GPA1/YHR005C locus on chromosome VIII of Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C.

  • BBa_26J53MF4 Basic part

    FUS1 promoter (435 bp)

    Promoter
    435 bp

    A 435 bp fragment of the FUS1 upstream region from Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C. FUS1 is one of the genes the mating pathway switches on, which makes its promoter a convenient readout: whatever activates the cascade upstream ends up written out here as transcription. This part is the promoter only — no reporter, no terminator, no backbone — so it can be paired with whichever output a construct needs.

    Source and composition

    Saccharomyces cerevisiae S288C chromosome III, NCBI RefSeq NC_001135.5, positions 71,368–71,802 on the forward strand.

  • BBa_266CQI7L Basic part

    S. cerevisiae α-factor pre-signal peptide

    Signal peptide
    57 bp

    The pre-region of the S. cerevisiae α-factor precursor, used as an N-terminal signal peptide to route a downstream fusion protein into the secretory pathway. In the PAGER receptor designs it carries the extracellular sensing module toward the plasma membrane, so the antigen-binding domain ends up facing the outside of the cell.

    Source and composition

    Derived from the S. cerevisiae α-factor (MFα1) pre-sequence.

  • BBa_26DI50C2 Basic part

    Ste2 antagonist peptide CRGPQLKPGQPMY

    Polypeptide
    39 bp

    Encodes the 13-residue Ste2 antagonist peptide CRGPQLKPGQPMY, designed from structure–activity studies of the α-factor–Ste2 interaction. It is the auto-inhibitory element of the PAGER-Ste2 design: without H1N1 HA it occupies the Ste2 ligand-binding region and suppresses basal signalling, and antigen binding to the fused nanobody is expected to relieve that inhibition.

    Source and composition

    Sequence taken from published structure–activity studies of α-factor analogues (doi:10.1128/MCB.16.9.4700; doi:10.1016/j.peptides.2003.11.028).

  • BBa_26J0YWR7 Basic part

    Flexible Gly-Ser linker (11 aa)

    Polypeptide
    33 bp

    A flexible Gly-Ser-rich linker placed between the extracellular regulatory module and hM1Dq. It gives the two domains conformational freedom and enough spatial separation to fold independently, while keeping steric interference with receptor function low.

    Source and composition

    Synthetic Gly-Ser linker, 11 residues.

  • BBa_26U5OU4I Basic part

    Anti-H1N1 HA nanobody

    Coding sequence
    342 bp

    A single-domain antibody that specifically recognises the haemagglutinin of influenza A H1N1. In the PAGER receptor it sits in the extracellular region and serves as the antigen-recognition module: HA binding is meant to relieve steric inhibition of the receptor and let the downstream pathway fire.

    Source and composition

    Nanobody raised against H1N1 haemagglutinin, codon-optimised for S. cerevisiae.

  • BBa_26I4T75I Basic part

    TEV protease cleavage site

    Protease site
    21 bp

    A TEV protease recognition site placed between the extracellular antigen-binding module and the receptor-associated domain. Cleaving it removes the upstream module, so receptor activation can be tested with and without the antigen-gated half in place.

    Source and composition

    Standard TEV protease recognition sequence (ENLYFQG).

  • BBa_2624TRUA Basic part

    ALFA-tag (13-aa epitope)

    Tag
    39 bp

    Encodes the 13-residue ALFA-tag, an epitope recognised by ALFA-specific antibodies and binding reagents. In the PAGER receptor it sits between the TEV site and the flexible linker and is used to confirm that the fusion is expressed and reaches the membrane.

    Source and composition

    Synthetic ALFA epitope tag.

  • BBa_266RB3HX Basic part

    Muscarinic toxin 1 (MT1) auto-inhibitory domain

    Coding sequence
    198 bp

    Muscarinic toxin 1, an extracellular auto-inhibitory domain used in the PAGERGq design. Fused to the N-terminus of hM1Dq it holds the receptor shut while no antigen is present; binding of the anti-H1N1 HA nanobody to its antigen is expected to relieve that block and let an orthogonal DREADD ligand activate hM1Dq.

    Source and composition

    Muscarinic toxin 1 from mamba venom, used here as a steric gate.

  • BBa_267G8SJJ Basic part

    Engineered hM1Dq receptor for yeast Gq signalling

    Coding sequence
    1038 bp

    The engineered hM1Dq DREADD receptor adapted for expression in S. cerevisiae. It is the transmembrane signalling core of the PAGERGq system: once MT1-mediated auto-inhibition is relieved by antigen binding, it activates Gq-coupled signalling in response to an orthogonal DREADD ligand. The part carries the receptor modifications used in this project.

    Source and composition

    Engineered from the hM1Dq DREADD receptor for yeast expression.

Composite Parts

The 6 assembled entries. Each one is a combination of parts from the section above or from the wider Registry, and each lists its components.

Part ID Name Type Length Function
  • BBa_26IKMSKO Composite part

    FAR1 sgRNA expression cassette (SNR52p–guide–scaffold–SUP4t)

    Engineered region
    388 bp

    A complete expression cassette that puts the FAR1 guide under RNA polymerase III control in yeast: the SNR52 promoter drives transcription, the 20 bp spacer confers specificity, the SpCas9 scaffold supplies the RNA structure Cas9 binds, and the SUP4 terminator ends the transcript. Together with the matching repair template it turns the target locus into a frameshift after a single Cas9 cut. The cassette is designed for the pML104 CRISPR-Cas9 system used throughout the chassis work.

    Source and composition

    Assembled from Registry parts plus a computationally designed FAR1 spacer.

    Construct
    1. BBa_K2926004
    2. BBa_262TZXV3
    3. BBa_267ZCE19
    4. BBa_K4278714
  • BBa_26YVX4QT Composite part

    SST2 sgRNA expression cassette (SNR52p–guide–scaffold–SUP4t)

    Engineered region
    388 bp

    The SST2 twin of the FAR1 sgRNA cassette, built from the same four Registry parts in the same order — SNR52 promoter, SST2 spacer, SpCas9 scaffold, SUP4 terminator — for RNA polymerase III expression in yeast. Paired with the SST2 repair template it gives a frameshift knockout after one Cas9 cut, and because the scaffold is shared with the FAR1 cassette the two targets differ only in their 20 bp spacers.

    Source and composition

    Assembled from four Registry parts; the SST2 spacer was designed computationally from the Saccharomyces cerevisiae SST2 locus.

    Construct
    1. BBa_K2926004
    2. BBa_2658B1ZA
    3. BBa_267ZCE19
    4. BBa_K4278714
  • BBa_266K1VDV Composite part

    GPA1 C-terminal humanisation cassette with URA3 selection marker

    Engineered region
    2280 bp

    The complete donor cassette for editing GPA1 in a single transformation. Running in order: the upstream homology arm, the humanised EYNLV sequence, the URA3 promoter, the URA3 gene, the URA3 terminator, a short connecting sequence, and the downstream homology arm. In BY4741, which is URA3 auxotrophic, the embedded URA3 cassette is what lets transformants be recovered on uracil-deficient medium, while the flanking arms alone decide where the construct lands.

    Source and composition

    Assembled from BBa_26HUBYAY, BBa_26EG2LOR, BBa_K3939012, BBa_Y1007, BBa_K2407302, a custom linker and BBa_26YEUKXX.

    Construct
    1. BBa_26HUBYAY
    2. BBa_26EG2LOR
    3. BBa_K3939012
    4. BBa_Y1007
    5. BBa_K2407302
    6. BBa_26YEUKXX
  • BBa_26D8D1SG Composite part

    FUS1 promoter–yEGFP reporter fragment

    Engineered region
    1152 bp

    A composite reporter that pairs the FUS1 promoter with the yEGFP reporter CDS. The promoter answers to the yeast mating-response MAPK cascade, so once the pathway is active it drives yEGFP transcription and translation and the cell fluoresces green. It is the output module used to characterise both the re-engineered chassis and the humanised G-protein interface.

    Source and composition

    Assembled from the truncated FUS1 promoter BBa_26J53MF4 and the yEGFP reporter CDS.

    Construct
    1. BBa_26J53MF4
    2. BBa_K3221207
  • BBa_26YO211V Composite part

    PAGER-STE2 H1N1 HA-gated yeast signalling module

    Engineered region
    623 bp

    The H1N1 HA-gated module that plugs into the native yeast Ste2 mating pathway. Reading N- to C-terminus it carries the α-factor pre-sequence, the Ste2 antagonist peptide, a flexible linker, the anti-H1N1 HA nanobody, a TEV site and an ALFA-tag. The nanobody provides HA recognition while the antagonist peptide gates Ste2 signalling; the Ste2 receptor itself is supplied by the chassis and is not part of this construct.

    Source and composition

    Assembled from BBa_266CQI7L, BBa_26DI50C2, BBa_26J0YWR7, BBa_26U5OU4I, BBa_26I4T75I and BBa_2624TRUA.

    Construct
    1. BBa_266CQI7L
    2. BBa_26DI50C2
    3. BBa_26J0YWR7
    4. BBa_26U5OU4I
    5. BBa_26I4T75I
    6. BBa_2624TRUA
  • BBa_26CKZT2G Composite part

    PAGERGq H1N1 HA-responsive engineered hM1Dq receptor

    Engineered region
    1740 bp

    The antigen-responsive receptor assembly of the PAGERGq design. The α-factor pre-sequence targets the construct to the secretory pathway, MT1 keeps the receptor auto-inhibited until antigen arrives, the anti-H1N1 HA nanobody provides recognition, the TEV site makes the gating module removable for controls, and the engineered hM1Dq receptor couples activation to the engineered Gpa1/Gαq interface. An ALFA-tag is carried for detection.

    Source and composition

    Assembled from BBa_266CQI7L, BBa_266RB3HX, BBa_26J0YWR7, BBa_26U5OU4I, BBa_26I4T75I, BBa_2624TRUA and BBa_267G8SJJ.
