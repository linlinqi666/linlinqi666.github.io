Parts

This page lists every biological part used in PAGER-Yeast, grouped into three functional modules: signal recognition, signal transduction optimisation, and signal reporter. Each part carries its identifier, name and description, with long sequences available to expand.本页列出 PAGER-Yeast 项目中使用的全部生物元件，按信号识别、信号转导优化、信号报告三个功能模块归置。每个元件给出编号、名称与描述，长序列可展开查看。
